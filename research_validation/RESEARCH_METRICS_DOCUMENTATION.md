# RESEARCH METRICS DOCUMENTATION

This document defines the calculations and sources of all quantitative metrics generated by the AI Hand Rehabilitation System.

> [!WARNING]
> Several metrics in this system are **heuristic**. They are mathematically consistent models designed to guide a software gamification loop, NOT clinically validated medical diagnostics.

## 1. Accuracy (`avg_accuracy`)
*   **Source:** `physio/game_parts/` (Runtime logic)
*   **Formula:** Percentage of targets successfully reached / Total targets presented.
*   **Range:** `0.0 - 100.0`
*   **Database:** `sessions.avg_accuracy`
*   **Validation Status:** Direct mathematical calculation (Valid).

## 2. Range of Motion (`avg_hand_angle` / `max_finger_extension`)
*   **Source:** `physio/hand_engine.py`
*   **Formula:** Trigonometric angle derived between MediaPipe wrist, MCP, and fingertip landmarks in 2D webcam space.
*   **Range:** `0.0 - 180.0` degrees
*   **Database:** `sessions.avg_hand_angle`
*   **Validation Status:** **Heuristic.** 2D webcam projection is subject to parallax error and does not perfectly map to a 3D clinical goniometer.

## 3. Pain Delta (`pain_difference`)
*   **Source:** `physio/game_parts/screen_parts/pain.py`
*   **Formula:** `pain_after_session - pain_before_session`
*   **Range:** `-10 to 10`
*   **Database:** `sessions.pain_difference`
*   **Validation Status:** Valid (Direct User Reported Input).

## 4. Readiness Score (`readiness_score`)
*   **Source:** `adaptive_ai_engine.py` (Line 424)
*   **Formula:** 
    `30 * (Accuracy/100) + 22 * (ROM/90) + 16 * ((10-Pain)/10) + 12 * Consistency + 10 * Smoothness + 6 * (1-Fatigue) + 4 * Quality`
*   **Range:** `0.0 - 100.0`
*   **Database:** `adaptive_prescriptions.readiness_score`
*   **Validation Status:** **Heuristic.** Arbitrary weighted gamification score. Not a clinical metric.

## 5. Compensation Score (`compensation_score`)
*   **Source:** `upper_limb_analysis.py` (Line 119)
*   **Formula:** `clamp(0.40 * shoulder_comp + 0.35 * elbow_comp + 0.25 * trunk_comp, 0.0, 1.0)`
*   **Range:** `0.0 - 1.0`
*   **Database:** `upper_limb_assessments.compensation_score`
*   **Validation Status:** **Heuristic.** Based on 2D MediaPipe Pose estimations. Highly sensitive to camera placement and distance.

## 6. Recovery Prediction Velocity
*   **Source:** `digital_twin_rehab.py` (Line 312)
*   **Formula:** `(Current ROM - Baseline ROM) / Days * 7.0` (Weekly ROM Velocity)
*   **Database:** `digital_twin_snapshots.recovery_velocity`
*   **Validation Status:** Mathematical trendline. Valid representation of the software's recorded data, but not a clinical prognosis.
